This collection includes adaptations of the following projects. Modifications are by Firepanda415.

Adapted in `benchmark-paper-template`, `tech-paper-template`, `figure-designer`, `pre-submission-reviewer`, and the proposal-evaluation reference in `develop-research-ideas`. Adapted in the copyediting reference of `research-writing-style`, with changes to preserve scientific meaning and author voice and support the collection’s editing conventions. The methodology draws on Kristin Sainani’s *Writing in the Sciences*.
- [simplify-codebase](https://github.com/tt-a1i/simplify-codebase), simplify-codebase contributors (2026), MIT. Adapted with scientific-evidence guidance and shorter reporting requirements.

Changes add scientific accuracy and resource requirements, integrate the hooks into this plugin, and update installation guidance.
