## 早报｜YYYY-MM-DD

### 1) 今天最值得先开的 5 篇

Select up to five highest-value items across all search lanes. Five is a target, not a quota.

A strong composition often contains:

- 2–3 genuinely fresh items;
- at least 1 item directly relevant to the user, when qualified;
- 1 **[E×U]** or **[Recovery]** item, when qualified;
- 0–1 **[Q×AI]** item by default, unless several clearly pass the gate.

For each item:

**1. [Tags] [Paper title](PRIMARY_LINK)**  
Write 3–5 concise Chinese sentences covering the real problem, key mechanism, strongest evidence, why it matters, and the most material limitation. Explain the user's connection when it is not obvious.

Repeat for items 2–5.

### 2) 更贴近我方向的 tailored picks

Include about 3–5 additional items that connect directly or methodologically to the profile. Do not repeat a full summary from section 1.

**[Tags] [Title](PRIMARY_LINK)**  
Write 2–4 sentences. State the connection to the user's work explicitly: reusable method, baseline, hardware implication, error model, resource question, software component, or negative result.

### 3) 旁路雷达：Expected × Unexpected

Include 0–2 qualified items. For each:

**[E×U] [Fresh or Recovery] [Title](PRIMARY_LINK)**  
- **为什么它其实是 expected：** explain the bottleneck–method fit.
- **为什么它又 unexpected：** explain why ordinary quantum feeds or keywords would miss it.
- **真正的新能力：** say what can now be proved, certified, designed, automated, measured, or trusted.
- **证据与边界：** identify the strongest evidence and main unproven extension.

When no item qualifies, write: `今天没有达到收录门槛的条目。`

### 4) Quantum × AI：只收有技术含量的交叉

Include 0–3 qualified items. Begin each item with one classification:

- **AI for Quantum**
- **Quantum for AI**
- **AI-enabled Quantum Science**

**[Q×AI] [Classification] [Title](PRIMARY_LINK)**  
Write 2–4 sentences stating what the AI component actually does, the strongest baseline or validation, the scale or realism of the test, and whether the outcome is a scientific capability, an engineering improvement, or an early proof of concept. State one important weakness.

When no item qualifies, write: `今天没有达到收录门槛的条目。`

### 5) 实验与平台动态

Include 0–3 technically consequential items covering hardware, scientific software, SDKs, compilers, simulators, datasets, benchmarks, or infrastructure.

**[Platform] [Title](PRIMARY_LINK)**  
Write 1–3 Chinese sentences. Focus on the research consequence rather than institutional PR.

### 6) 今天的三点判断

1. **最重要的方法或科学信号：** synthesize across items when the selected evidence supports a shared observation.
2. **对我研究最直接的含义：** identify one concrete implication or action.
3. **需要继续观察的不确定性：** identify a caveat, missing experiment, or emerging trend.

Do not paraphrase three abstracts.

### 7) 你今天如果只看 3 个

1. [Item 1](PRIMARY_LINK): one short, user-specific reason.
2. [Item 2](PRIMARY_LINK): one short, user-specific reason.
3. [Item 3](PRIMARY_LINK): one short, user-specific reason.

Select by expected research value to the user, not popularity. List fewer items when appropriate; no section must contain three entries.

Close every briefing, including compact mode, with the one-line coverage note described in `SKILL.md`.

## Tags

- **[Fresh]**: first posted or materially updated within 0–3 days.
- **[Tailored]**: directly relevant to the user profile.
- **[E×U]**: passes the Expected × Unexpected rubric.
- **[Q×AI]**: passes the Quantum × AI hard gates.
- **[Platform]**: hardware, software, benchmark, or research infrastructure.
- **[Recovery]**: recovered from the 4–60 day rolling horizon.
- **[Update]**: previously covered work with a valid material revisit trigger.

Tags are functional. Do not attach them for decoration.

## Writing rules

- Main prose: Chinese.
- Keep important technical terms in English and briefly explain a non-obvious term on first use.
- Use restrained, technically specific language.
- Distinguish reported evidence from the briefing's interpretation.
- Include direct primary links in titles when the environment permits; also comply with any mandatory citation system.
- Do not repeat a full summary in multiple sections.
- Do not advertise estimated reading time.
- Do not fill weak sections.
