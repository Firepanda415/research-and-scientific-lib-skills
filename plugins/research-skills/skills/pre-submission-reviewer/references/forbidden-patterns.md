# Presentation and house-style checks

## House style

The user avoids em dashes in authored research prose. Preserve verbatim quotations, code identifiers, old-text anchors, and bibliographic titles. Apply the other explicit vocabulary and punctuation preferences in `research-writing-style` when producing or revising text.

These are editorial requirements. An em dash or a repeated word is not automatically a Major scientific finding. Do not infer AI authorship from vocabulary, sentence shape, or a detector score.

Judge wording by meaning. Words such as "general-purpose", "yielding", "reveal", "robust", and "significant" can be precise technical language. Flag a phrase only when it is unsupported praise, vague, redundant, or changes the claimed scope. No occurrence-count thresholds are required.

## Overclaims

Compare the claim with its actual support. A statement of superiority needs a defined metric, comparison set, regime, and uncertainty where relevant. A novelty claim needs a defensible prior-work boundary. "Comprehensive experiments" is not a substitute for stating what those experiments establish.

Severity follows the consequence. A local adjective can need an editorial correction; an unsupported central advantage claim can require Major or Critical revision. Do not infer the stronger failure from the adjective alone.

## Figures

Check whether ranges, truncation, scale, normalization, selected instances, and visual emphasis alter the reader's interpretation. Use `figure-designer` for quantitative encoding and rendering details. Decoration is usually polish unless it obscures or distorts evidence. Inspect an available figure rather than requiring the user to describe it.

## Attribution

Check a specific suspected overlap against the source before making an allegation. Distinguish conventional phrasing, attributed quotation, properly disclosed reuse, and unsupported claims of originality. Similarity alone does not establish plagiarism. Do not silently rewrite quoted source material or manufacture a misconduct finding from a phrase match.

## Output

List relevant issues with exact evidence and proportionate consequences. Keep house-style corrections separate from scientific concerns. A dedicated full-paper style scan is useful when requested, not a prerequisite for answering an unrelated scientific question.
